# Job Application Helper

**Job Application Helper** is a minimal yet powerful AI-powered web app built to simplify and strengthen your job applications.
It analyzes your resume and any job description, extracts key skills, compares them side by side, and instantly generates a personalized AI cover letter, all within seconds.

---

## Features

* **AI Cover Letter Generator**
  Generates confident, tailored cover letters using **Gemini Flash 2.5**, perfectly aligned with your resume and job description.

* **Smart Skill Extraction**
  Extracts skills directly from your uploaded resume (via **pdf.js**) and parses job description text for relevant keywords.

* **Skill Comparison Table**
  Displays a clear, side-by-side table of **Matching Skills** and **Missing Skills**, giving you a real sense of readiness for each role.

* **Match Percentage Score**
  Calculates how well your resume aligns with the target job posting.

* **Privacy First**
  All data is processed locally in your browser — **no uploads, no tracking, no storage**.

## 💡 How It Works

1. **Upload your resume (PDF).**
2. **Paste a job description.**
3. The system extracts and compares skills.
4. **View a detailed table** of matching and missing skills.
5. Get your **AI-generated cover letter** instantly — ready to copy, tweak, and send.
